# Banking guidance

This directory contains maintained operational guidance used by the durable
[Banking module requirement](../../project/requirements/features/009-bankingModule.md).
Guidance is separated by jurisdiction because rules, terminology, thresholds
and provider processes change independently of the domain model.

## United Kingdom

- [Deposit protection](uk/depositProtection.md)
- [Bereavement](uk/bereavement.md)
- [Power of attorney and other representatives](uk/powerOfAttorney.md)
- [Joint accounts](uk/jointAccounts.md)

Every guide states its jurisdiction, effective/verified date, next review date
and official sources. Stored guidance supports preparation and workflow prompts;
users must verify current institution and official requirements before acting.
