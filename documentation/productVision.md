# Product vision

## A family should not need one person to explain everything

Every household depends on knowledge that is rarely gathered in one place.
Some of it is formal: insurance, care arrangements, finances, legal documents
and health information. Much of it is ordinary but essential: who can look
after a pet, where the stopcock is, how school routines work, which bills need
attention and where important records can be found.

Often one person carries a disproportionate share of that knowledge. If they
are seriously ill, lose capacity or die, the people around them must make urgent
decisions while grieving, worried or exhausted. They may know that information
exists without knowing where it is, whom to contact or what needs to happen
next.

clanneolas.com exists to make that situation less confusing.

## The problem we are solving

People need a practical way to organise the knowledge their family may depend
on when they cannot manage things themselves. Existing information is commonly
scattered across memory, paper files, email, online accounts, phones and
conversations. Conventional emergency or estate-planning lists can also miss
the everyday family knowledge that keeps a household functioning.

The answer is not to collect every sensitive detail into one dangerous file.
It is to help people record the right information at the right level: useful
instructions, trusted contacts and safe references to where protected records
or access arrangements can be found.

## Our vision

We want any household to be able to create a clear, safe and maintainable
handbook that helps trusted people understand what matters and what to do next.

The handbook should be:

- understandable without specialist knowledge;
- useful on paper and offline;
- careful about privacy and proportionate about personal data;
- broad enough to include care, home life and family knowledge as well as
  financial and legal administration;
- adaptable to different households, countries and circumstances;
- built from open, human-readable formats; and
- usable without a vendor account or future clanneolas.com application.

Success is not measured by how much information a household records. It is
measured by whether trusted people can find reliable guidance when it matters,
without the handbook creating unnecessary risk.

## Why this project is different

### The handbook comes before software

This is a handbook project first. Software may eventually make some tasks
easier, but the core product must never depend on an application, subscription
or online service. A printed or locally stored handbook should remain useful
for as long as its content is maintained.

### Privacy is part of the content design

We do not treat privacy as a warning added at the end. Templates should ask only
for information with a clear purpose. Passwords, PINs and recovery codes do not
belong in ordinary handbook content. Sensitive records should normally be
referenced by location rather than copied.

### Everyday knowledge matters

Families depend on more than accounts and official documents. Recipes,
traditions, care preferences, household routines and the small practical facts
known by one person can be just as difficult to recover. The handbook gives
that knowledge a deliberate place.

### Public resources and private lives stay separate

The repository contains reusable guidance, blank templates and clearly
fictional examples. A family's completed handbook is private household data and
does not belong in the public project.

### The core can travel

Initial guidance may reflect the United Kingdom, but family responsibilities
are not confined to one jurisdiction. Country-specific terms and processes
should be separable from the core model so contributors can adapt the handbook
without rebuilding it.

## A promise to contributors

Contributions should make a difficult future moment more manageable for real
people. That means choosing clarity over cleverness, restraint over unnecessary
data collection and durable usefulness over novelty.

The project is still at an early stage. The current repository contains an
outline and proposed requirements, not a finished handbook or application.
Contributors have the opportunity to shape its foundations carefully—and a
responsibility not to describe intentions as completed work.

The [project personas](personas/personasIndex.md) turn this vision into concrete,
fictional situations that requirements and design decisions can be tested
against.
